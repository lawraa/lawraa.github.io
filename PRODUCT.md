## Platform

web

## Stack

React 19 + Vite + Tailwind v4 + react-router (HashRouter), deployed as a static GitHub Pages user site at https://lawraa.github.io/. Leaflet powers the bouldering map.

## Purpose

Personal portfolio of Shou-Jen (Lawrance) Chen: Software Development Engineer at Amazon in Seattle since Sep 2026 (describe the role by that title only); UC Berkeley EECS MEng (robotics and embedded systems) and NTU Electrical Engineering undergrad. The site presents ML, robotics, and LLM work, publications, a resume PDF, and two personal pages (bouldering, drawing).

## Audience

Industry hiring (ML / robotics / AI engineer roles) and research readers (professors, labs) weigh equally. Success for industry: they understand the depth of the engineering and reach out or download the resume. Lawrance works at Amazon, so the site does not present them as job hunting. Success for research: they read the papers and the case-study write-ups.

## Content and evidence

- Publications: LLM Discussion (COLM 2024, co-first author), Dynamic-SUPERB Phase-2 (ICLR 2025, co-author), IEEE SLT 2024 reviewer.
- Projects with long case studies (problem, approach, architecture, results, reflection) in `src/data/projects.ts`, many with real figures, PDFs, and a quadruped control video in `public/projects/`.
- Experience: Berkeley MEng capstone (LLM-driven assistive grasper design, report PDF; never submitted to a venue, so never claim a submission), TrendForce, Pegatron, IADIY Photonics, NTU Robot Learning Lab, NTU SPML Lab, NTU WMN Lab. The JPMorgan Forage simulation appears only as a project, not as experience.
- Personal: bouldering (V8 peak, gym map, photos, Instagram @climblog.lawry), drawing (pencil studies).
- Portrait at `public/lawry/portrait.jpg`. Resume PDF at `public/resume.pdf` (the user maintains it; there is no separate resume page, since the PDF and the home experience list cover it).

## Constraints

- All five routes (Home, Projects, Publications, Bouldering, Drawing) are in scope for the redesign; none of the current visual identity, copy, or components is a brand commitment.
- No invented claims: metrics, venues, and dates come only from the data files.

## Brand commitments

- Polished classic over concept: a familiar portfolio structure (bio and portrait, selected work, experience, contact) executed with high craft, not a themed metaphor. Confirmed by the user after four direction rounds (2026-10-23).
- A small palette of 3-4 colors used across sections and tags; not monochrome, not color-drenched.

## Voice

First person, plain and specific, technically precise. Say what was built and with what; no slogans, rhetorical questions or "not just X but Y" constructions (the user rejected these as sounding AI-written, 2026-10-24).
